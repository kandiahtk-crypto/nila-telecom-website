Help centre

Good to know.

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into compatible phones and devices. It allows a mobile plan to be installed without inserting a traditional plastic SIM card.

Can I keep my normal SIM?

On compatible dual-SIM devices you can usually keep your existing SIM and use an eSIM for travel data. Device capabilities vary, so check your phone before purchasing.

When should I install my eSIM?

Installation and activation rules can vary by plan. Follow the instructions supplied with your Nila eSIM before travelling.

Does every phone support eSIM?

No. Your device must support eSIM technology and normally needs to be network-unlocked. Check your device specifications before buying.
